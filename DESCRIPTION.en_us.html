Alisia is a contemporary humanistic sans-serif typeface engineered specifically for extended, fatigue-free reading across digital screens and print.

Drawing inspiration from modern geometric sans-serifs and neo-grotesque discipline, Alisia introduces generous sidebearings to eliminate visual crowding, softened curvature transitions on lowercase shoulders (n, m, h, u), open apertures (c, e, s, a), and a natural 265-unit word space that creates effortless saccadic scanning across continuous text.

Alisia comes in three carefully calibrated weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), and Bold (700), supporting Western European and Latin character sets.
