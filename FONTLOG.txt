FONTLOG for the Alisia font family

This file provides detailed information on the Alisia font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Alisia fonts
and any derivative works.

Basic Font Information

Alisia is a contemporary, humanistic sans-serif typeface designed for
extended, fatigue-free reading across digital screens and print. Featuring
gentle curves, open apertures, generous sidebearings, and natural
saccadic rhythm.

ChangeLog

22 Nov 2026 (FoxHound-X & Setia AI) Alisia v1.000
- Initial release under SIL Open Font License v1.1.
- Project repository: https://github.com/FoxHound-X/alisia-font
- Engineered 3 weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700).
- Calibrated zero-crowding sidebearings (+16 units) and 265 units word space.
- Full OpenType CFF, TrueType, and Brotli WOFF2 webfont builds.
- Complete Google Fonts submission bundle (METADATA.pb, DESCRIPTION, OFL).
